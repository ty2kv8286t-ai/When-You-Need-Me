For My Favorite Person

If you're reading this, I know you probably miss me... so I made this little place for you to come whenever you need me.

My love,

If you ever miss me so badly that you wish I could magically appear beside you, I want you to come here and remember that somewhere out there is a girl who loves you more than she could ever put into words.

I wish I could be there right now. I wish I could pull you into my arms, hold you as tight as I could, lay beside you, play with your hair, and remind you that everything is going to be okay.

I know the distance isn't always easy. Sometimes I wish we didn't have to count the hours until we could see each other again. But even with all those miles between us, nothing about the way I love you has gotten smaller.

If anything, the distance has made me realize just how much you mean to me.

You aren't just my girlfriend. You're my best friend, my favorite person, my safe place, and the person I want beside me through everything.

I still think about how everything started between us. Who would've thought something as simple as a blue Powerade would lead me to the person I would eventually fall completely in love with?

And then there was April 5th... the day I got to officially call you mine.

I would choose that day over and over again.

Every time I look at you, I swear I fall in love with you all over again. Your ocean-blue eyes, your brown hair, your smile, your laugh, the way you talk, the way you care... all of it.

You brought a side of me back that I didn't even know I was missing. You brought a kind of happiness into my life that I didn't know I could feel.

So when you miss me, please don't only think about the distance.

Think about us.

Think about every laugh, every late-night conversation, every little memory, every kiss, every "I love you," every moment where we realized just how much we mean to each other.

And remember that there is someone who would give anything just to hold you right now.

Me.

No matter how far away you are, you're still my person. You're still my girl. You're still the one I want.

And one day, I won't have to miss you from this far away. I'll get to hold you whenever I want, kiss you whenever I want, fall asleep beside you, wake up beside you, and make all these miles between us disappear.

Until then, just know that my heart is with you. Always.

I love you more than words could ever explain. ❤️

Forever yours,
Your girl ❤️
